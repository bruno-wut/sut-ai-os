# Product

Sri U-Thong Grand Hotel AI OS is a governed, hotel-owned operating layer for direct commerce, hotel operations, growth intelligence, technical reliability, and controlled AI automation.

Its purpose is to improve direct bookings and operational quality through a closed loop: observe signals, measure deterministically, interpret evidence, authorize safely, execute bounded work, verify outcomes, and retain an audit trail.

The product is not a replacement PMS, unrestricted database assistant, autonomous revenue manager, content farm, or all-powerful agent. Pricing, inventory, payments, guest data, financial records, production database changes, and consequential approvals remain controlled by deterministic systems and authorized humans.
