# Owner Launch Content Sign-off

Do not approve production until every item is confirmed for both English and
Thai where applicable. This checklist is intentionally factual: it does not
authorize inventing room, venue, or legal information.

## Rooms and Media

- [x] Final public room names and room-type mapping
- [x] Guest-facing room descriptions, occupancy, bedding, sizes, amenities, and rates
- [ ] Approved photography for hero, rooms, dining, meetings, gallery, location, and contact pages
- [ ] Accurate alt text for all approved images
- [ ] Confirmation that no placeholder or stock-description copy remains

## Meetings, Dining, and Contact

- [x] Venue names, capacities, layouts, equipment, and event contacts
- [x] Restaurant name, hours, breakfast details, inclusions, and dining copy
- [x] Address, phone, email, LINE, Agoda, Facebook, and official booking URL

## Legal and Localization

- [x] PDPA privacy policy, booking terms, and cancellation/refund policy approved
- [x] Fluent Thai translations approved, including legal and checkout text
- [x] English public copy approved
- [x] Thai and English mobile review completed at 320px, including Trirong/Noto ascenders and descenders

## Launch Evidence

- [ ] `npm run verify:storefront:freeze` passes on the release candidate
- [ ] Staging screenshots/URLs show no placeholder copy or broken media
- [ ] Owner name, approval date, and release commit SHA are recorded in the launch decision log
