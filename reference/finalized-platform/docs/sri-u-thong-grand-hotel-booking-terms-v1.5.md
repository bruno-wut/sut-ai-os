## 2. Electronic Reservation Mechanics & Transaction Protection

### 2.1 The 35-Minute Inventory Hold

Selecting a room tier initializes a secure database reservation hold ("Hold") for a maximum duration of 35 minutes. This framework is implemented to maintain inventory availability, prevent overbookings, and guard against automated site scrapers.

The Guest must complete payment authorization via our integrated payment gateway before this 35-minute window expires.

If the secure transaction sequence is not finalized within this timeframe, the inventory hold will release automatically, and the unreserved rooms will return to the public booking matrix.

### 2.2 Operational Confirmation

For operational and evidentiary purposes under the Thai Electronic Transactions Act B.E. 2544 (2001), a reservation is recognized as confirmed when the Platform emits an authorized booking confirmation record following transaction verification. The Platform will immediately transmit a structured booking confirmation receipt containing a unique alphanumeric reservation reference code (e.g., [R-XXXX]) to your verified email address.

### 2.3 Payment-Success Safeguard

If funds are successfully debited or authorized by the payment gateway (e.g., Stripe or Opn) from your account, but the Platform fails to generate a confirmation receipt due to a network delay, system disruption, or webhook delivery failure, the Guest will not be penalized. In such circumstances:

- The Hotel will manually confirm the intended booking if the inventory category remains open; or
- If the inventory category has filled during the system delay, the Hotel will provide an equivalent or higher room upgrade, or return the full debited amount to the Guest through the original payment channel.

### 2.4 Data Minimization and PDPA Integrity

Upon expiry of an unpaid Hold, the Platform releases inventory automatically and deletes, anonymizes, or minimizes temporary personal data that is no longer necessary for fraud prevention, payment reconciliation, system security, audit history, or legal compliance, in accordance with the Thai Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) and the Hotel's Privacy Policy.

## 5. Cancellation, Amendment, and Refund Procedures

### 5.1 Staff-Mediated Cancellation Requirements

The Platform’s guest lookup interface provides a read-only booking status summary and does not permit direct guest-side cancellation or modification. To request an amendment or cancellation, the Guest must contact the Hotel directly by telephone or by submitting a written request from the registered email address, or another email address reasonably verified by the Hotel, to reservations@sriuthonghotels.com.

### 5.2 Standard Cancellation Window

To receive a full refund for a standard refundable reservation, subject to Clause 5.4, the Guest’s cancellation request must be received by the Hotel at least 1 full day before the scheduled check-in date, calculated using the Hotel’s local timezone, Asia/Bangkok, ICT, UTC+7. For example, if check-in is scheduled for a Friday, the cancellation request must be received by 11:59 PM ICT on the preceding Wednesday. The Hotel may require reasonable identity or booking verification before processing the cancellation, but refund eligibility will be assessed based on the timestamp when the cancellation request was first received, provided the Guest supplies the information reasonably required to identify the booking.

### 5.3 Late Cancellation Charge Cap

Cancellations requested outside the standard 1-day window may result in a late cancellation charge. This fee is strictly capped at the lower of: (a) the deposit paid; or (b) the cost of the first night’s room charge, inclusive of applicable service charge and VAT, unless the booking was explicitly completed under a clearly labeled non-refundable promotional rate plan.

### 5.4 Payment Gateway Processing Fees

Any non-refundable merchant gateway processing fees assessed by the payment provider will be explicitly disclosed to the Guest on the payment interface prior to final transaction execution.

### 5.5 Manager Review for Late or Exceptional Cancellations

Cancellations or amendments requested close to the scheduled check-in time may require manager or administrator review before approval, especially where charges, refunds, room release, or operational exceptions are involved.

### 5.6 Refund Timelines

Once a qualifying refund request is approved, the Hotel will submit the refund request to the payment gateway within 7 to 14 business days. The exact time required for the credit to appear on the Guest’s statement depends on the processing cycles of the card issuer, payment provider, or financial institution.

## 6. No-Show and Late Arrival Management

### 6.1 Automated Rollover Clock

To protect open property distribution pipelines, rooms are secured for Guests throughout their scheduled arrival date. If a Guest fails to check in at the property and does not notify the property of a late arrival by 4:00 AM ICT on the morning following the scheduled arrival date, the reservation status will transition automatically within our system to a No-Show.

### 6.2 Inventory Handling and No-Show Caps

Following a No-Show system change, the Hotel is not required to continue holding the room for the remainder of the reservation timeline, and the room inventory is released back to the public pool. In compliance with the Thai Civil and Commercial Code regarding proportional damage assessments, the no-show charge is strictly limited to the first night's room charge or the deposit paid, whichever is lower, unless the reservation was purchased under a clearly marked non-refundable rate plan. For multi-night standard refundable bookings, release of remaining nights does not create an additional charge beyond the no-show cap stated above, unless a clearly marked non-refundable rate applies.

**Cash Deposits:** Returned in full at check-out immediately following a successful physical room inspection, minus any verified charges.

**Credit Card Authorization Holds:** Released by the Hotel system at check-out. The Guest's issuing bank may take additional business days to clear the hold from the available balance.

### 8.3 Onsite Charges Settlement

All extra-person or extra-bed fees will be clearly displayed in the room details or checkout page. If a Guest arrives with more occupants than permitted by safety parameters, the Hotel may require the booking of an additional room. All auxiliary charges or onsite incidentals incurred during your stay cannot be processed via the online IBE and must be settled directly at the front desk before check-out.
