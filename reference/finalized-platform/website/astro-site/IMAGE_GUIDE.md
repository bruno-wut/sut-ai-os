## Photo Direction

- Prioritize honest, well-lit photos over over-edited luxury imagery.
- Keep rooms tidy and realistic.
- For hero images, leave enough calm space for text overlays on the left or lower portion.
- Avoid dark, blurry, heavily cropped, or low-resolution photos.
- Use consistent color temperature across the gallery where possible.

## Section-by-Section Crops

The dimensions above describe source files. The table below describes how each image is actually used in the layout, so the photographer or editor can prepare crops without losing important details on mobile.

| Section | Display Shape | Focal-Point Requirement | Responsive Safe Area |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home hero | Full-bleed landscape, approximately 16:10 on desktop and tall 4:5 on mobile | Place the hotel arrival, entrance, or strongest architectural feature slightly right of centre. | Keep the left 42% visually quiet for the headline. Keep key signage and faces out of the outer 12% on all sides. |
| Interior page heroes | Full-bleed landscape, approximately 2:1 on desktop and 4:5 on mobile | One clear subject with enough environmental context to identify the room, restaurant, event space, or location. | Keep the lower-left quadrant calm for title, description, and buttons. Do not place faces or signage beneath this overlay area. |
| Home introduction | Portrait 4:5 | Lobby or reception scene with a clear vertical composition and natural depth. | Keep the main subject within the centre 60%; this image becomes wider on smaller screens. |
| Home room cards | Landscape 4:3 | Bed and window or the clearest room-defining feature. | Keep the bed and major furniture within the centre 70%; avoid important details at the top and bottom edges. |
| Rooms listing | Wide landscape 5:3 | Show the full bed zone plus enough floor area to communicate space. | Keep the primary bed or seating group within the centre 70%; allow both left and right crops. |
| Dining cards | Landscape 4:3 | Use one distinct scene per card: breakfast service, restaurant interior, and group table. | Keep food, place settings, and people within the centre 70%; avoid cropped hands or faces near edges. |
| Meetings and events cards | Landscape 4:3 | Show room scale, seating layout, and a clear aisle or visual anchor. | Keep the central setup unobstructed; allow the outer 15% to crop on narrow screens. |
| Gallery thumbnails | Landscape 4:3 | Each frame needs a single readable subject even before it opens. | The full source opens in the viewer, but the central 70% must still work as a thumbnail. |
| Location content | Landscape 4:3 or 3:2 | Prefer an honest arrival, frontage, or recognisable nearby context image. | Keep signs and the main entrance inside the centre 65% so they survive responsive crops. |
| Contact hero | Full-bleed landscape with a warm arrival or lobby scene | Choose a welcoming human-scale scene, ideally reception or lobby seating. | Keep the lower-left quadrant calm for contact actions and retain a clear pathway into the scene. |

## Delivery Checklist

- Supply one master image per filename at the recommended source size; do not upscale smaller originals.
- Export JPG at roughly 75–85% quality or WebP at an equivalent visual quality.
- Keep each large hero file below about 500 KB where practical and content images below about 350 KB.
- Preserve natural colour and straight vertical lines; avoid heavy HDR, orange colour casts, or artificial blur.
- Remove visible guest personal information, payment screens, licence plates, and private event signage.
- Confirm room names and event-space names before embedding them in image metadata or captions.
- Provide photographer credit and usage rights separately if attribution or licence restrictions apply.
